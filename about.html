WHY THIS WEBSITE?

MasterText is an educational website designed to support students in developing their English language and writing skills. It provides a learning space where students can strengthen their understanding of written communication and become more confident in expressing themselves.

The website was created in response to the difficulties students may experience when learning English, particularly when organising their ideas, understanding different forms of writing and communicating effectively.

MasterText aims to make learning more accessible and manageable by bringing useful educational support into one online space. It encourages students to take an active role in their learning and continue developing their abilities at their own pace.

The website is mainly intended for students who want to improve their English, including those who need additional guidance, those preparing for school assessments and those who simply want to become better writers.

Ultimately, MasterText aims to help students become more independent, capable and confident communicators, both inside and outside the classroom.
