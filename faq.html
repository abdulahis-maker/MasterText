Frequently Asked Questions

What is MasterText?

MasterText is a learning website designed to help students improve their English writing through examples, guides, tips, and practice activities.

Who can use MasterText?

MasterText is designed for students who want to develop their English writing skills and become more confident writers.

What can I learn on this website?

You can explore model answers, practice questions, English guides, tips and tricks, and writing challenges.

How do I improve my writing?

Read the model answers, follow the English guides, practise different text types, and use the tips to improve your writing.

Can I practise different types of writing?

Yes. MasterText provides practice questions and model answers to help you understand how to write different text types.

What are the Challenges?

Challenges give you opportunities to test your writing skills and apply what you have learned.

Can I save my writing?

The Drafts section helps you keep your writing organised and return to unfinished work.

How can I share a suggestion?

Visit the Contact page to send a suggestion or message about how MasterText could be improved.
